# Basin City

A small mining town in Nevada, abandoned after the resonance experiment. The town sits on the Nevada Thrust geological formation, which creates a natural quantum lens that amplifies quantum energy.

## Description

Basin City was a thriving mining community before the resonance experiment. The town's economy was based on the extraction of rare earth minerals from the Nevada Thrust formation. When Xenex Corporation built Research Facility 7 on the outskirts of town, the community welcomed the jobs and investment.

After the incident, the town was evacuated and sealed. The government classified the area as a restricted zone, and the buildings were left to decay. The streets are empty, the windows dark, the doors locked.

## Role in Story

Basin City is the focal point of the quantum resonance. The Nevada Thrust formation beneath the town acts as a natural quantum lens, focusing and amplifying the entity's signal. The thin spot in the quantum barrier is located here, and the distributed quantum channel runs through the geological formation beneath the town.

Maya travels to Basin City to investigate the resonance pattern and ultimately closes the thin spot by activating the facility's quantum regulators.
