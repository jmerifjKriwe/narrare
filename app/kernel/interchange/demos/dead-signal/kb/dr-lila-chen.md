# Dr. Lila Chen

Lead quantum physicist on the resonance project at Research Facility 7. Maya's younger sister. Officially deceased, but her consciousness persists in the quantum space between moments.

## Background

Lila Chen was a prodigy. She earned her doctorate in Quantum Physics at 22 and was recruited by Xenex Corporation for the resonance project at 25. She believed the experiment could revolutionize human understanding of reality. She was right—but not in the way she expected.

## Personality

Lila was curious, brave, and relentlessly optimistic. Where Maya was practical and skeptical, Lila was theoretical and open-minded. She saw patterns where others saw noise, connections where others saw chaos. She was the one who first detected the entity's signal, and the one who advocated for communication rather than containment.

## Role in Story

Lila is the catalyst for everything that happens. Her message from beyond the barrier sets Maya on her journey. Her research provides the tools to understand and ultimately close the thin spot. And her transformation—becoming something that exists in the space between moments—represents the entity's ultimate goal: not destruction, but evolution.

## Key Dialogue

*"Maya. If you're receiving this, the barrier is stable enough. I'm still here. We all are. But the connection is fading, and there's something I need you to know. Director Kavic lied. The experiment didn't fail. It succeeded too well. We're not dead, Maya. We're just... somewhere else."*
