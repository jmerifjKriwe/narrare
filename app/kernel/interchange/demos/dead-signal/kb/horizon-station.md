# Horizon Station

A research outpost at the edge of explored space, studying phenomena that can't be observed closer to home.

## Description

Horizon Station is a rotating cylinder design, half a kilometer long, housing laboratories, living quarters, and the most sensitive quantum sensors humanity has ever built. It's located in the Oort Cloud of a dying star—chosen because quantum anomalies are more common in regions of high gravitational stress.

The station is remote. At 12 light-years from Earth, communication is delayed by years each way. The crew is isolated, dependent on each other and the station's systems for survival.

## Sections

**Command Deck**: Maya's office, ARIA's core, station operations. The nerve center.

**Research Labs**: Biology, physics, quantum mechanics—all the science that brought the crew here.

**Hydroponics**: The station's garden, providing both food and psychological relief. Crewmate Torres's domain.

**Living Quarters**: Small but functional rooms. Maya's is full of technical journals and coffee mugs.

**Engineering**: The station's guts—reactors, life support, ftl drive maintenance. Maya's second home.

**Observation Deck**: A transparent section at the station's axis, providing a view of the stars that never gets old.

## Atmosphere

Horizon Station is quiet. The hum of systems, the whispered conversations of crew members passing in corridors, the background music that ARIA selects to match the mood. It's peaceful in the way that only true isolation can be.

But it's also fragile. Life support is a continuous process. One mistake, one system failure, and everyone dies. The crew lives with this knowledge, always aware of how thin the line is between routine and emergency.

## Role in Story

Horizon Station is Maya's refuge and her prison. She came here to escape Earth and its memories. The station is her world, her family, her identity. When the signal arrives, it doesn't just threaten her understanding of physics—it threatens the sanctity of the home she's built.

The station's isolation is also key to the mystery. When Maya receives the signal, she can't just call someone for answers. She has to figure it out herself, with ARIA's help and the resources at hand.

## Theme

Horizon Station represents the human drive to explore, to understand, to push boundaries. It's also a testament to human resilience—the ability to create home and family in the most unlikely places. The signal threatens everything the station represents, forcing Maya to choose between her refuge and the truth.
