# Lins Armband

A personal identification device worn by Dr. Lila Chen. The armband modulates at 14.2 Hz, serving as Lila's unique quantum signature. The entity uses this frequency to identify Lila among billions of humans.

## Description

Lins Armband is a simple wristband containing a quantum resonance crystal. The crystal is tuned to 14.2 Hz, Lila's personal identification frequency. The armband was designed by Lila herself, as part of her research into quantum identification systems.

When Lila was pulled into the space between moments, the armband went with her. Its quantum signature became the entity's way of identifying Lila—and, by extension, of identifying humanity. The 14.2 Hz frequency became the entity's calling card, its way of saying "I am here, and I know who you are."

## Role in Story

Lins Armband is the key to the entity's communication. The 14.2 Hz frequency is how the entity identifies Lila, how Lila's signal is transmitted, and how the quantum channel is established. Without the armband's unique frequency, the entity would have no way to distinguish Lila from any other human.

The armband also serves as a plot device: when Maya detects the 14.2 Hz signal, she knows it's from Lila. When the entity projects itself in Lila's form, the 14.2 Hz frequency is present. And when the quantum channel is established, the 14.2 Hz modulation persists as a background hum—a reminder that Lila is still out there.
