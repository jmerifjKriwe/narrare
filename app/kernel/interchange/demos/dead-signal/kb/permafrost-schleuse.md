# Permafrost-Schleuse

A maintenance airlock between Sub-basement Level 3 and Level 4 of Research Facility 7. The site of a confrontation between Maya Chen and an entity-projected figure resembling Dr. Lila Chen.

## Description

The Permafrost-Schleuse is a standard industrial airlock, designed for personnel movement between levels. It was sealed after the incident and remained unused for three years. The airlock's name comes from the extreme cold of the surrounding rock—the Nevada Thrust formation maintains a constant temperature of 4°C at this depth.

## Role in Story

The Permafrost-Schleuse is where Maya confronts the entity directly. The entity projects itself in the form of Dr. Lila Chen, using her quantum signature as a template. The confrontation reveals the entity's true nature: not hostile, but curious and lonely, seeking understanding rather than conquest.

The confrontation also reveals that the quantum window Maya created is not a single point but a distributed channel, running through the entire Nevada Thrust formation. The entity is no longer concentrated at a single location—it is spread across hundreds of kilometers of crystalline rock.
