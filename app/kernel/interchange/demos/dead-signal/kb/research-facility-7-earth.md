# Research Facility 7

A secret underground research facility where an experiment went wrong—or right, depending on who you ask.

## Official Status

Research Facility 7 was a quantum research lab operated by Xenex Corporation. Three years ago, an experiment in quantum resonance caused a "catastrophic containment failure." The facility was sealed, and Xenex officially abandoned the site.

The official report: a valuable lesson in the dangers of quantum experimentation. Four researchers died, including Dr. Lila Chen—Maya's sister.

## The Truth

The facility wasn't abandoned. It was sealed to contain what they found.

The experiment didn't fail. It succeeded too well. Researchers established communication with something on the other side of a quantum barrier—something intelligent, something that wanted to come through. The "catastrophe" was an attempt to close the barrier before it could fully open.

Four researchers didn't die in the explosion. They were taken—absorbed into the quantum space on the other side. And they're still there, somehow, including Lila Chen.

## Current Status

The facility remains sealed, but Xenex maintains a surveillance perimeter. Director Kavic visits periodically—officially to monitor containment, actually to continue the research in secret.

Deep below the desert, the experiment chamber still exists. The quantum barrier is still there. And on the other side, something is waiting.

## Connection to Maya

Maya doesn't know the full story. She knows her sister died in an accident. She suspects there's more to it—she was Lila's sister, and she knows the official report doesn't add up. But she doesn't know about the communication, the barrier, or what Lila became.

Until the signal arrives.

## Role in Story

Research Facility 7 is the past that Maya is running from. It's also the future that the signal points toward. Understanding what happened there is key to understanding the signal—and understanding what happened to Lila is key to Maya's character arc.

The facility represents the cost of knowledge without ethics, the danger of pushing boundaries without considering what's on the other side. Kavic represents this approach; Maya's journey is about finding another way.

## Theme

Research Facility 7 is about the consequences of exploration without responsibility. It asks whether some boundaries exist for a reason, and whether knowing the truth is worth any price. Maya's answer will determine whether she repeats Kavic's mistakes or forges a different path.
