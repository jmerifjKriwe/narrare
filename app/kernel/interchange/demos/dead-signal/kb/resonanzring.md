# Resonanzring

A quantum resonance device built at Research Facility 7. The Resonanzring creates and studies quantum resonance patterns, and was the instrument that first made contact with the entity existing in the quantum foam.

## Description

The Resonanzring is a ring of crystalline structures, each tuned to a specific quantum frequency. When activated, the ring creates a resonance pattern that extends into the quantum foam, allowing communication with entities existing beyond normal spacetime.

The device was designed by Dr. Lila Chen and built by Xenex Corporation. It was intended as a research tool, but Director Kavic saw it as a weapon and a source of power.

## History

The Resonanzring was activated for the first time six years ago. The experiment was a success—the team made contact with the entity. But Kavic attempted to contain the entity, to trap it for study. The containment failed, the quantum barrier collapsed, and four researchers were pulled into the space between moments.

After the incident, the device was moved to a sub-basement lab. Kavic continued to study it, trying to reactivate it. Three years later, the device's residual quantum activity created a thin spot in the barrier, and the entity began to communicate through the signal.

## Current Status

The Resonanzring is still active, though in standby mode. Maya Chen activated the quantum regulators, closing the thin spot and establishing a permanent quantum channel. The device continues to function as a window between worlds, allowing limited communication between the entity and humanity.
