# Signal-Modulation 14.2 Hz

The quantum frequency at which Dr. Lila Chen's identification signal modulates. This frequency serves as a unique signature, allowing the entity to identify and communicate with Lila specifically.

## Description

The 14.2 Hz frequency is not a radio frequency or an electromagnetic wave. It is a quantum vibration—a pattern in the quantum foam that serves as an identification marker. The entity uses this frequency to distinguish Lila from other humans, and to establish a quantum channel for communication.

When the entity began broadcasting through the barrier, the signal modulated at 14.2 Hz. This was how Maya identified the signal as coming from Lila—the frequency matched Lila's quantum signature, which was on file at Research Facility 7.

## Role in Story

The 14.2 Hz modulation is the key to the entire mystery. It is how Maya knows the signal is from Lila, how the entity identifies Lila among billions of humans, and how the quantum channel is established and maintained.

The frequency also appears in the entity's distributed communication pattern, running through the Nevada Thrust formation. Even after the thin spot is closed, the 14.2 Hz modulation persists as a background hum—a reminder that Lila is still out there, somewhere in the spaces between moments.
