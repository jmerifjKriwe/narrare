# Station 7/9 Diskrepanz

A data anomaly discovered by Maya Chen during her investigation of the signal. The discrepancy between Station 7 (Research Facility 7) and Station 9 (a secondary monitoring station) reveals that Director Kavic has been covering up the true nature of the resonance experiment.

## Description

Station 7 was the primary research facility where the resonance experiment took place. Station 9 was a secondary monitoring station, located fifty kilometers away, designed to track quantum anomalies in the surrounding area.

Maya discovered that Station 9's logs showed a 41% loss of quantum connection to the sub-space node during the incident—significantly more than Station 7's official report indicated. This discrepancy suggested that the incident was more severe than Kavic had reported, and that the entity's influence extended far beyond the facility.

## Role in Story

The Station 7/9 Diskrepanz is a key plot point that reveals Kavic's deception. By comparing the two stations' data, Maya discovers that Kavic has been manipulating the records to downplay the severity of the incident and conceal the entity's true nature.

The discrepancy also provides evidence that the entity's influence is not limited to the facility—it extends across the entire Nevada Thrust formation, affecting quantum conditions for hundreds of kilometers.
