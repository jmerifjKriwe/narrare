# Sub-Orbitaldock

A docking facility on Horizon Station used for shuttle launches and arrivals. The Sub-Orbitaldock is where Maya Chen's journey begins and ends—her departure point for Basin City and her return point after closing the thin spot.

## Description

The Sub-Orbitaldock is a small but well-equipped facility on the station's outer hull. It houses two shuttle bays, a maintenance workshop, and a control room. The dock is connected to the station's main systems by a series of corridors that pass through the engineering section.

## Role in Story

The Sub-Orbitaldock serves as the departure and arrival point for Maya's journey to Basin City. It is also where ARIA monitors the quantum channel, detecting the entity's communications and logging all activity for analysis.
