# Xenex Corporation

A multinational corporation specializing in offworld operations, research, and experimental technology. Xenex Corporation funded and operated Research Facility 7, and employed both Dr. Lila Chen and Director Kavic.

## Background

Xenex Corporation was founded as a mining company, extracting rare earth minerals from asteroids and distant planets. Over time, it expanded into research, technology development, and offworld operations. The corporation's most ambitious project was the resonance experiment at Research Facility 7, which was intended to revolutionize quantum communication and FTL travel.

## Role in Story

Xenex Corporation is the institutional antagonist of the story. The corporation's pursuit of profit and power—embodied by Director Kavic—drives the conflict. Kavic's decision to contain the entity, to trap it for study, is motivated by corporate ambition, not scientific curiosity.

After the incident, Xenex Corporation continued to operate, covering up the true nature of the experiment. It was only after Kavic's arrest and the public revelation of the entity that the corporation was dissolved. Its assets were seized, its research was classified, and its employees were scattered.
