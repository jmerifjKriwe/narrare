# Aethelgard

The capital and largest city of the kingdom, built on the ruins of the old capital destroyed in the Void Cataclysm.

## Description

Aethelgard is a city of contrasts. The wealthy districts near the base of Silver Flame Fortress feature elegant stone buildings, well-paved streets, and Order mages providing magical services. The outer districts are a maze of timber-framed buildings, crowded markets, and desperate poverty. Everywhere, the Order's presence is felt—patrols, propaganda, and the ever-glowing fortress watching from above.

## Districts

**The High Quarter**: Nobility, wealthy merchants, high-ranking Order officials. Clean, safe, and heavily patrolled.

**The Market District**: Commercial heart of the city. Everything can be bought here for the right price. Sera's shop, "The Curious Squirrel," is located here.

**The Artisan Quarter**: Craftspeople, workshops, and the magical component market (heavily regulated by the Order).

**The Docks**: Trade hub along the River Aethel. Also home to smugglers and black market operations.

**The Shadows**: The poorest district. Overcrowded, underpatrolled, and home to the city's underground network.

## Atmosphere

The city bustles with energy and underlying tension. Order propaganda painted on walls reminds citizens that "Magic Controlled is Safety Guaranteed." In taverns and private homes, voices lower when discussing the Order. Most citizens accept the Order's rule as necessary—but an undercurrent of resentment grows.

## Order Presence

- **Patrols**: Order guards and mages patrol all districts, but frequency varies by district
- **Registration Centers**: All mages must register at local Order offices
- **Propaganda**: Posters, criers, and required education emphasize the dangers of uncontrolled magic
- **Surveillance**: Order informants operate throughout the city

## Underground Network

Beneath the streets, in back rooms and hidden basements, a network of dissidents operates:
- **Safe Houses**: Hidden locations where fugitive mages can find temporary shelter
- **Information Networks**: Sera and others like her track Order movements and share information
- **The Ashen Covenant**: The organized resistance has cells throughout the city
- **Smugglers**: Move forbidden texts, magical components, and people in and out of the city

## Role in Story

Aethelgard provides the setting for Elara's interactions with the world beyond the fortress. Through Sera and the underground, she learns that the Order's narrative isn't the whole story. The city represents the ordinary people affected by the Order's policies—some supportive, some resentful, most just trying to survive.
