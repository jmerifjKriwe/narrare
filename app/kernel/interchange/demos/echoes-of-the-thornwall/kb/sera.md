# Sera

The owner of "The Curious Squirrel" shop and a member of the Ashen Covenant who helps fugitive mages.

## Background

Sera was born to merchant parents in Aethelgard's Market District. When her void magic manifested at age fourteen, she hid it—knowing the Order would execute or "disappear" her. For years, she lived a double life: respectable shopkeeper by day, secret void practitioner by night. When the Ashen Covenant found her, she didn't join immediately. But after seeing the Order execute a street child for "uncontrolled magic," she chose sides.

## Appearance

- Height: 5'4" (163 cm)
- Hair: Dark brown, cropped short
- Eyes: Sharp brown, miss nothing
- Build: Unremarkable—easy to overlook, which serves her well
- Attire: Practical merchant clothing, never Order robes

## Personality

Sera is pragmatic, cynical, and deeply protective of fugitive mages. She doesn't trust easily—years of hiding will do that—but once earned, her loyalty is absolute. She has a dry sense of humor and a talent for appearing harmless while gathering intelligence. Her shop is a front; her real work is the Covenant.

## Magic

**Void Affinity**: Like Elara, Sera is void-sensitive. She has developed her abilities in secret over fourteen years, learning techniques the Order claims are impossible.

**Combat Capabilities**: She avoids direct confrontation but can defend herself if necessary. Void manipulation allows her to create pockets of vacuum, bypass barriers, and escape through spaces that shouldn't exist.

## Role in Story

Sera is Elara's introduction to the truth behind the Order. Through Sera, Elara learns that:
1. The Order's narrative about the Cataclysm is incomplete
2. Void magic can be controlled safely
3. There is an organized resistance working against the Order

Sera represents what Elara could become—a void mage living outside the Order's control, using her abilities to help others.

## Relationships

- **Elara**: Sera sees potential in Elara and carefully guides her toward the truth. She doesn't pressure Elara to join the Covenant, trusting that the truth will lead her there eventually.
- **Kaelen**: Sera tolerates Kaelen because he's Elara's friend, but she doesn't trust him. His loyalty to the Order makes him dangerous, even if he doesn't realize it.
- **The Ashen Covenant**: Sera is an active member, serving as an information broker in the capital.

## The Shop

"The Curious Squirrel" sells herbs, minerals, and magical components—many of them technically legal but requiring permits the Order rarely grants. It also serves as a meeting place for Covenant members and a safe house for fugitive mages.

## Quote

*"The Order doesn't protect people from magic. They protect their own power. There's a difference."*
