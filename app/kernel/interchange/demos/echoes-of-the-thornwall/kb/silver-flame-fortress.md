# Silver Flame Fortress

The imposing headquarters of the Order of the Silver Flame, overlooking the capital city from its mountain perch.

## Description

Carved into the side of Mount Silver, the fortress has been the Order's seat of power for three centuries. Its white stone walls seem to grow from the mountain itself, seamless and imposing. By day, sunlight makes the fortress gleam like its namesake. By night, magical flames burn along its parapets, casting an eerie glow visible for miles.

## Layout

**Outer Ward**: Training grounds, barracks, support facilities. Where initiates and guards live and train.

**Inner Sanctum**: Administrative offices, the Council Chamber, and High Chancellor Morven's quarters. Access restricted.

**Archive Tower**: The Order's library, containing thousands of texts on magic. Restricted sections on upper floors.

**The Pit**: An underground detention facility where "dangerous" mages are held. Few know what happens below the third level.

**Temple of the Flame**: The spiritual heart of the fortress. Order ceremonies and executions take place here.

## Atmosphere

The fortress is a place of hushed voices, rigid discipline, and constant vigilance. Initiates move with purpose, eyes averted from superiors. The air smells of incense, ozone from magic practice, and an underlying hint of fear.

## Security

- **Magical Wards**: The fortress is protected by layers of defensive magic, keyed to Order members' magical signatures.
- **Physical Defenses**: A full garrison of trained guards, plus combat-capable mages.
- **Surveillance**: Scrying orbs monitor public areas. Private conversations are rarely private for long.
- **The Eye of Morven**: High Chancellor Morven's personal scrying ability allows him to monitor many parts of the fortress.

## Hidden Areas

**Level 4 of The Pit**: Officially does not exist. Rumors speak of experimental magic on prisoners—testing methods of suppressing or removing magical ability.

**Secret Archives**: Below the Archive Tower, texts deemed too dangerous for general access are stored—including original documents from before the Cataclysm that contradict Order doctrine.

## Role in Story

For Elara, the fortress is home, school, and prison all at once. She knows its corridors, routines, and secrets better than anywhere else. But as her power grows and her loyalty wavers, the fortress transforms from sanctuary to threat.

## Plot Hooks

- Elara must sneak into the Secret Archives to learn about the Thornwall relic
- Kaelen is assigned to patrol duty, creating moral dilemmas if he discovers Elara's disobedience
- Master Verrick uses his position to facilitate Elara's access to restricted areas
- The Pit becomes a looming threat—if Elara's void magic is discovered, she could end up there
