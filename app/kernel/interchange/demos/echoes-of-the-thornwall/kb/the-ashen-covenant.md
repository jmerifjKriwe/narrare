# The Ashen Covenant

A growing rebel movement opposing the Order's harsh policies and seeking magical freedom.

## Origins

Fifteen years ago, a group of mages escaped from Order custody and formed a resistance. They called themselves the Ashen Covenant—a reference to the "burned" state of magic under Order rule. What began as a small survival group has grown into an organized movement with cells throughout the kingdom.

## Philosophy

**Core Belief**: Magic is a natural part of the world and should be understood and worked with, not suppressed. The Order's methods create more problems than they solve.

**Goals**:
- Overturn the Order's totalitarian control
- Establish free magical practice
- Rediscover lost knowledge from before the Cataclysm
- Create a society where mages and non-mages coexist without fear
- Expose the Order's corruption and the truth about the Cataclysm

**Methods**:
- Propaganda and information campaigns to counter Order narratives
- Rescue operations for mages hunted by the Order
- Research into forbidden magic (void and otherwise)
- Sabotage of Order operations when necessary
- Building alliances with non-mages who resent Order control

## Structure

The Covenant operates in cells to minimize the risk of exposure:

**Central Leadership**: A council of founding members and newer leaders. Coordinates strategy and major operations.

**Cell Leaders**: Each city has one or more cells operating independently. Leaders know only their direct contacts.

**Sympathizers**: Many more people support the Covenant than actively join. Sera is one such sympathizer—she provides resources and information but isn't a member.

**Allies**: Some nobles and even Order members secretly support the Covenant. The Order's methods have made enemies across society.

## Activities

**Information Network**: The Covenant maintains an extensive intelligence network, tracking Order movements and finding mages who need help.

**Safe Houses**: Hidden locations throughout the kingdom provide shelter for fugitives.

**Research**: Covenant scholars recover and study texts from before the Cataclysm, seeking knowledge the Order suppresses.

**Rescue Missions**: The Covenant regularly breaks mages out of Order custody or prevents their capture.

**Propaganda**: Pamphlets, whispers, and public demonstrations challenge Order narratives.

## Challenges

**Resources**: The Covenant operates on a shoestring, relying on donations and raided supplies.

**Security**: Order infiltrators are a constant threat. Cells are regularly compromised.

**Disunity**: Not all members agree on methods. Some want peaceful reform; others advocate violent revolution.

**Public Opinion**: Many citizens still believe Order propaganda. The Covenant struggles to win hearts and minds.

## Relationship with Elara

The Covenant sees Elara as a potential prize—her void magic makes her powerful, and her position within the Order makes her valuable. But they also respect her choice. Unlike the Order, the Covenant believes in free will. They will offer Elara a place, but they won't force her to join.

Key Covenant members Elara encounters:
- **Joren Ashwell**: Sera's brother, a cell leader in Aethelgard
- **Master Lyra**: A former Order master who defected and now leads Covenant research
- **The Silent Keeper**: Temple guardian who secretly supports the Covenant

## Future

The Covenant is growing stronger each year, but so is the Order's response. A confrontation is coming—one that will determine the future of magic in the kingdom. Elara's choice will tip the balance.
