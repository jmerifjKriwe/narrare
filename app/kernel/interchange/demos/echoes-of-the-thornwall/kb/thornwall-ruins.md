# Thornwall Ruins

The remains of an ancient academy, destroyed during the Void Cataclysm forty years ago. Now a forbidden zone, shrouded in magical distortion.

## Description

Thornwall was once the premier magical academy in the kingdom, known for innovative research into all forms of magic—including void magic. Now it's a landscape of broken towers, collapsed halls, and overgrown ruins. But the physical destruction isn't the most dangerous part.

## Magical Distortion

The Cataclysm that destroyed Thornwall left permanent scars on reality itself:

- **Spatial Warping**: Distances shift unpredictably. A straight line might curve back on itself.
- **Time Dilation**: Time moves differently in different parts of the ruins. Hours inside might be minutes outside, or vice versa.
- **Void Resonance**: The original void magic that caused the Cataclysm still lingers, causing hallucinations, attracting void-sensitive creatures, and creating pockets of unstable reality.
- **Memory Echoes**: The magical backlash imprinted fragments of the Cataclysm onto the location. Visitors sometimes see ghosts of the past—images of mages fleeing, towers collapsing, reality unraveling.

## The Forbidden Zone

The Order maintains a perimeter around Thornwall:
- **Patrols**: Order guards prevent unauthorized entry
- **Wards**: Magical barriers detect and deter intruders
- **Propaganda**: Tales of Thornwall emphasize its danger to keep curiosity-seekers away
- **Research Ban**: Officially, all research into Thornwall is forbidden

However, the Order secretly conducts expeditions into the ruins, seeking artifacts and knowledge from before the Cataclysm.

## What Really Happened

**The Official Story**: Void magic research went wrong, causing a catastrophic reality collapse that destroyed the academy and half the capital. This story is largely true—but incomplete.

**The Hidden Truth**: Thornwall's researchers were working on a way to harness void magic for beneficial purposes—dimensional travel, communication across vast distances, healing by manipulating the fundamental structure of reality. The Cataclysm wasn't malicious research gone wrong; it was sabotage. Someone within the academy intentionally destabilized the void experiments, killing hundreds and setting back magical understanding by decades.

**The Motive**: Evidence suggests that the sabotage was orchestrated by early Order founders, who used the Cataclysm to justify their rise to power and the strict control of magic that followed.

## The Relics

Thornwall produced several powerful magical artifacts before its destruction. The Order has recovered some, but others remain hidden:
- **The Thornwell Compass**: Found by Elara. Points toward void magic signatures and can stabilize spatial warping.
- **The Void Mirror**: Allows communication across dimensions and viewing of distant places. Rumored to still exist in the ruins.
- **The Reality Anchor**: A device that can stabilize or destabilize reality in an area. Its location is unknown.

## Role in Story

Thornwall represents the truth behind the Order's propaganda. It's dangerous, yes—but also the key to understanding what void magic really is and what it could become. For Elara, whose magic is naturally drawn to void energy, Thornwall is both threat and opportunity.
