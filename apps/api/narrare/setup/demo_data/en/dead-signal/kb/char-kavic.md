# Director Kavic

The corporate director who funded Horizon Station and has a personal interest in what Maya discovers.

## Background

Kavic rose through Xenex Corporation's ranks through a combination of technical competence and ruthless pragmatism. He oversees all offworld operations—research stations, mining outposts, experimental facilities. Horizon Station is his most remote and most important project, though few people know why it's important.

## Personality

Kavic is not evil by his own lights. He's a businessman doing what's necessary for progress. He believes that knowledge belongs to those who can use it, that secrets are meant to be exploited, and that the good of the many (corporate profitability, which equals human advancement in his view) outweighs individual concerns. He's polite, professional, and utterly without moral qualms about doing what's "necessary."

## Motivations

**Public**: Xenex Corporation is expanding human knowledge through cutting-edge research. Horizon Station is at the frontier of human exploration.

**Private**: Kavic knows something about quantum resonance that he's not sharing. He funded Horizon Station specifically because of its location—at a coordinate where quantum anomalies are more likely. Maya's signal isn't just interesting science; it's something he's been waiting for.

## Methods

Kavic doesn't make threats. He makes offers. He doesn't issue orders; he makes requests that feel like choices. He understands power and how to wield it without appearing to. Crew members who don't cooperate find their careers stalled. Those who do find unexpected opportunities. No laws are broken—merely bent until they serve his purposes.

## Secrets

**The Quantum Resonance Project**: Three years before Maya arrived at Horizon Station, Kavic supervised an experiment at Earth-based Research Facility 7. Something happened. Officially, it was a failure that resulted in the facility's closure. Unofficially, Kavic has been searching for what was lost ever since.

**Maya's Sister**: Maya's sister wasn't just a random victim of an accident. She worked for Kavic. She was involved in the experiment. And Kavic knows exactly what happened to her—he just doesn't see why Maya needs to know.

## Role in Story

Kavic represents the choice between knowledge and ethics. He's not wrong that the signal could revolutionize human understanding. He's just wrong about the cost being acceptable. His conflict with Maya is about whether some truths are worth any price.

## Key Dialogue

*"Dr. Chen, you're a scientist. You understand that knowledge isn't good or evil—it just is. What matters is how we use it. And I'm offering you the chance to use it for the greatest good imaginable."*

*"At what cost?"*

*"Progress always has a cost. The question is whether you're brave enough to pay it."*

---

*Kavic is the mirror image of Maya—someone who chose knowledge over ethics and believes he made the right choice. His role is to test whether Maya will make the same choice.*
