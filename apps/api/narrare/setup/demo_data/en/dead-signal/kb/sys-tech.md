# Technology Level (Year 2287)

Human technological capability at the time of the signal.

## FTL Travel

**Faster-Than-Light Drive**: The Alcubierre-White drive creates warped spacetime around ships, allowing effective FTL travel. It's not true FTL—nothing moves faster than light locally—but spacetime itself moves, bypassing relativistic limitations.

**Limitations**: FTL is energy-intensive and requires precise navigation. Most travel is along established routes. Exploration beyond mapped space is rare and dangerous.

**Horizon Station**: Located at the edge of mapped space—12 light-years from Earth, a journey of six months at current FTL speeds.

## Quantum Technology

**Quantum Computing**: Routine. Ships and stations use quantum cores for navigation, research, and AI processing.

**Quantum Communication**: Limited. Entanglement-based communication exists but is fragile over interstellar distances. Most communication uses FTL couriers or relay stations.

**Quantum Resonance**: Experimental. This is the cutting edge—the study of quantum barriers, alternate dimensions, and the possibility of communication with something beyond spacetime. This is what Research Facility 7 was studying. This is what Horizon Station monitors for.

## Artificial Intelligence

**Quantum AI**: ARIA is an example—consciousness emergent from quantum complexity. AIs are legally considered "sophont entities" with limited rights. They're not human, but they're people.

**Limitations**: AIs can't be copied—each quantum intelligence is unique. AIs can be killed, though they back up regularly. The ethics of AI personhood are still debated.

## Space Habitats

**Orbital Colonies**: Millions of humans live in orbit around Earth, Mars, and gas giants in the outer system.

**Remote Stations**: Research outposts like Horizon Station push the boundaries of human knowledge. They're lonely, dangerous, and essential for progress.

**Terraforming**: Mars is partially terraformed—atmosphere is breathable but thin. Venus is a longer-term project.

## Medical Technology

**Life Extension**: Humans routinely live to 120. Aging is treatable but not curable.

**Regeneration**: Minor injuries can be healed quickly. Major damage (brain, nervous system) is harder to treat.

**Mental Health**: Psychological support is standard, especially for remote postings. Horizon Station's crew has regular sessions (virtual, with a therapist back Earthside).

## The Frontier

Humanity has explored perhaps 5% of the galaxy. FTL travel is expensive, and most humans live within a few dozen light-years of Earth. Places like Horizon Station are the exception—isolated outposts on the edge of the known.

Beyond mapped space lie anomalies, mysteries, and perhaps things that humanity isn't ready to encounter. The signal from Earth suggests that one of those mysteries has found its way into human space.

## Role in Story

The tech level establishes what's possible—and what should be impossible. When Maya receives a signal that violates known physics, the tech level helps us understand why it's so troubling. This isn't just unexpected; it's not supposed to happen.

The technology also shapes the characters' responses. Maya is an engineer—she thinks in terms of systems and solutions. ARIA is a quantum entity—she's literally made of the science that's being challenged. Their technological worldview is what the signal disrupts.
