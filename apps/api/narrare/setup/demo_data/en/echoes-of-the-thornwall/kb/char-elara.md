# Elara Thornwind

A young mage with forbidden void magic, struggling between her loyalty to the Order and her growing power.

## Background

Elara was orphaned at age seven when her village was destroyed by a magical cataclysm. The Order of the Silver Flame took her in, recognizing her magical potential. She trained as an initiate for twelve years, learning to suppress her natural affinity for void magic—a forbidden art that caused the very disaster that destroyed her home.

## Appearance

- Height: 5'6" (168 cm)
- Hair: Dark auburn, usually bound in Order braids
- Eyes: Gray with violet flecks (signs of void affinity)
- Distinguishing mark: Spiral scar on left palm from her first uncontrolled void surge

## Personality

Elara is deeply conflicted. She craves the Order's approval but resents their restrictions on her magic. She's intelligent, observant, and occasionally reckless when frustrated. Her fear of her own powers wars with her growing realization that the Order may not have her best interests at heart.

## Magic

**Void Affinity**: Elara can manipulate the empty spaces between things, creating pockets of vacuum, bypassing barriers, and—unbeknownst to her—potentially unraveling the fabric of reality itself.

**Current Abilities**:
- Small-scale vacuums (can extinguish flames, remove air from small spaces)
- Short-distance teleportation through void (exhausting, unreliable)
- Enhanced perception of magical signatures

**Forbidden Knowledge**: The Order's archives contain texts on advanced void magic, but access is restricted.

## Relationships

- **Master Verrick**: Her mentor at the Order. Strict but seemingly protective. Elara trusts him implicitly—perhaps too much.
- **Kaelen**: A fellow initiate who has shown interest in her beyond friendship. Elara is oblivious to his advances.
- **High Chancellor Morven**: Leader of the Order. Elara fears him and his increasing inspections of the initiates.
- **Thornwall relic**: Discovered during her expedition to the Thornwall ruins. It resonates with her void magic in ways she doesn't understand.

## Secrets

- Elara has been practicing void magic in secret against Order rules
- She experienced a vision when touching the Thornwall relic that she hasn't reported
- Her void surges are becoming more frequent and harder to control
