# Master Verrick

A high-ranking member of the Order who secretly questions Morven's methods and guides initiates toward their own moral choices.

## Background

Verrick joined the Order twenty years after the Cataclysm, drawn by their mission to protect the kingdom from magical threats. A brilliant student, he rapidly advanced through the ranks. However, over three decades of service, he has grown increasingly disillusioned with Morven's leadership. He remembers a time when the Order focused on education and protection rather than control and elimination.

## Appearance

- Height: 5'11" (180 cm)
- Hair: Salt-and-pepper, kept short
- Eyes: Warm brown, often thoughtful
- Attire: Standard Order robes, but with subtle modifications for comfort
- Distinguishing feature: Always carries a worn leather notebook

## Personality

Verrick is a teacher at heart. He is patient, thoughtful, and genuinely cares about his students' wellbeing. Unlike many Order officials, he believes that mages can be taught to control dangerous magic rather than being suppressed or eliminated. He is deeply conflicted about his secret sympathies—feeling like a coward for not openly opposing Morven, but knowing that open opposition would cost him his ability to help initiates from the inside.

## Magic

**Light Magic**: Verrick specializes in illumination, illusions, and defensive barriers. His magic is subtle but refined.

**Teaching Approach**: He emphasizes understanding and control over raw power. He is one of the few masters who will quietly answer questions about forbidden magics if asked in private.

## Relationships

- **Elara**: His most promising (and worrying) student. He sees himself in her and is secretly guiding her toward making her own choices rather than blindly following Order doctrine.
- **High Chancellor Morven**: Publicly loyal, privately opposed. Verrick carefully walks a line, doing enough to avoid suspicion while subtly undermining Morven's extreme measures when possible.
- **Kaelen**: Another initiate under Verrick's supervision. He sees Kaelen's loyalty to the Order as both admirable and potentially dangerous.

## Secret Activities

- **Information Leaking**: Verrick occasionally warns initiates before Order inspections
- **Archive Access**: He has facilitated unauthorized access to restricted texts for select students
- **Rebel Contacts**: While not officially a member, Verrick has met with Ashen Covenant members and understands their goals
- **Moral Guidance**: He encourages initiates to think critically about Order directives rather than obeying blindly

## Internal Conflict

Verrick knows that his secret help puts him at risk. Each year, he considers leaving the Order to join the rebels openly. Each year, he stays, telling himself that he can do more good from within. The appearance of the Thornwall relic and Elara's growing power may force him to finally choose a side.

## Quote

*"The Order was founded to protect mages as well as protect people from mages. Somewhere along the way, we forgot the first part of that mission."*
