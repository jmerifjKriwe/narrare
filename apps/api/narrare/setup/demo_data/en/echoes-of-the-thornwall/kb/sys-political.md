# Political Structure of Aethelgard

The government system of the kingdom and how the Order of the Silver Flame fits within it.

## The Monarchy

**King Aldric III**: The current monarch, elderly and increasingly dependent on the Order for magical services.

**Royal Authority**: In theory, the king has absolute power. In practice, the Order's influence means that High Chancellor Morven often dictates magical policy regardless of royal preference.

**Succession**: The king's heir, Prince Edric, is more skeptical of the Order but must be cautious about opposing them openly.

## The Order's Role

**Official Position**: The Order is an autonomous organization with authority over all magical matters within the kingdom. The crown granted this charter forty years ago and has never revoked it.

**Practical Power**: The Order controls:
- All magical education and training
- Enforcement of magical laws
- Research into magical phenomena
- Consultation to the crown on magical matters
- A private army (Enforcers)

**Legal Authority**: Order tribunals handle crimes involving magic. Their decisions cannot be appealed to civil courts.

## Tension Points

**Jurisdictional Disputes**: Civil authorities sometimes resent Order interference in non-magical matters. The Order claims that anything potentially involving magic falls under their purview.

**Economic Competition**: Order businesses provide magical services that compete with non-magical alternatives. Some merchants feel this is unfair competition.

**Noble Resistance**: Some noble families chafe under Order restrictions on their use of private mages. Wealthy families historically kept court mages; now all mages must serve the Order.

## The Underground

**Smugglers**: Move magical goods without Order approval. The Order actively hunts them, but demand ensures they continue operating.

**Fugitive Mages**: Unregistered mages who live in hiding. Some are harmless; others are genuinely dangerous. The Order uses both groups as justification for their strict policies.

**Ashen Covenant**: The organized resistance. The Order knows they exist but hasn't found their central leadership.

## Future of the Kingdom

The current situation is unstable. The Order grows more powerful each year, while resentment builds among those who chafe under their control. Several factors could trigger change:
- Death of King Aldric III (succession crisis)
- Discovery that the Order lied about the Cataclysm
- Open rebellion by the Ashen Covenant
- Noble coalition against Order authority

Elara's discovery of the truth could be the spark that ignites any of these powder kegs.
