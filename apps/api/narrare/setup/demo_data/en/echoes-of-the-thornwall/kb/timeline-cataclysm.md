# Timeline of the Void Cataclysm

The events leading up to, during, and following the magical catastrophe that reshaped the kingdom.

## Before the Cataclysm

**VC -5**: Thornwall Academy reaches its peak. Under the innovative Archmage Solara, the academy pushes the boundaries of magical research, including experiments with void magic. The academy's motto: "Understanding through exploration."

**VC -3**: Archmage Solara assembles a team to develop practical applications for void magic. Their goal: dimensional travel, instantaneous communication, and healing by manipulating reality's fundamental structure. The team includes young apprentices who will later found the Order of the Silver Flame.

**VC -2**: Early experiments succeed. The team demonstrates limited teleportation and creates the first void compass (later known as the Thornwell Compass). The academy celebrates these breakthroughs.

**VC -1**: Tensions emerge within the research team. Some members want to proceed cautiously; others push for more ambitious experiments. Archmage Solara argues for patience and continued research. A faction led by Morven (then known as Master Morven) advocates faster progress and military applications.

**VC -6 months**: Solara receives warnings from anonymous sources about sabotage within the team. She increases security but doesn't fully grasp the threat.

**VC -1 month**: The research team achieves their breakthrough: a stable dimensional portal. The success is celebrated, but Solara notices anomalies in the portal's readings. She recommends delaying further experiments until the anomalies are understood. Morven and his faction oppose delay.

## The Cataclysm

**VC Day**: The dimensional portal is activated for a full-scale demonstration. Unknown to most, Morven's faction has tampered with the portal's stabilizers, intending to cause a contained failure that will discredit Solara's leadership and give them control of the research.

**The sabotage goes wrong**: Instead of a controlled failure, the portal collapses catastrophically. The unleashed void energy expands exponentially, consuming Thornwall Academy and half the capital city. Reality itself begins to unravel.

**Casualties**: Over 10,000 people die in the initial collapse, including Archmage Solara and most of the research team. The death toll would rise to over 50,000 from aftereffects.

**The Collapse Spreads**: For three days, void storms sweep outward from the epicenter. Space twists, time fractures, and entire districts vanish. The kingdom's other magical academies combine their power to create a containment barrier.

**The Sacrifice**: A group of the kingdom's most powerful mages—mostly from the research team that survived the initial collapse—give their lives to seal the breach. Their sacrifice stops the collapse but leaves permanent magical scars on the land.

## Aftermath

**VC +1 week**: The immediate crisis ends, but the damage is unprecedented. The capital is half-gone. Thornwall Academy is a ruined landscape of spatial distortion and void resonance. The death toll continues to rise from void-related injuries.

**VC +1 month**: The first version of the Order of the Silver Flame forms from survivors of the research team. They claim the Cataclysm was caused by "irresponsible void magic research" and advocate strict control of all magic. Morven emerges as leader, using his role in the sacrifice (partially fabricated) to build authority.

**VC +6 months**: The Order officially forms with royal charter. King Aldric I, desperate for order and protection, grants the Order sweeping authority over all magical matters. The Order's narrative becomes the official history: the Cataclysm was caused by dangerous research, and only strict control can prevent another.

**VC +1 year**: The Order begins the Registration Act. All mages must register with the Order and undergo testing. Unregistered mages are declared criminals. The Order begins hunting "dangerous" mages—mostly void users.

**VC +2 years**: The Order establishes its headquarters in Silver Flame Fortress. Construction begins on the Pit detention facility. Propaganda campaigns remind citizens of the Cataclysm's dangers and the Order's protective role.

**VC +5 years**: The Order's authority is uncontested in magical matters. Resistance is minimal and disorganized. The Order begins rewriting history—destroying texts from before the Cataclysm, suppressing contradictory accounts, and elevating Morven to near-mythical status.

## The Rise of Resistance

**VC +15 years**: The first organized resistance forms. A group of escaped mages calls themselves the Ashen Covenant and begins rescuing others from Order custody. The Covenant is small but growing.

**VC +20 years**: The Temple of Silent Flames goes into complete hiding, severing all public contact. The Silent Keeper, a former Thornwall student, preserves knowledge from before the Cataclysm.

**VC +25 years**: Public unrest grows. The Order responds with harsher measures—more patrols, more executions, stricter propaganda. This creates more rebels, creating a cycle of repression and resistance.

**VC +30 years**: King Aldric II dies. His son, Aldric III, inherits a throne with diminished power and an Order that effectively runs the kingdom.

**VC +40 years (Present Day)**: The Order remains dominant but increasingly unstable. The Ashen Covenant has grown into an organized resistance. Public opinion is shifting. Whispers of the Cataclysm's true cause spread despite Order suppression. Elara Thornwind, a nineteen-year-old initiate with void magic, finds an artifact from before the Cataclysm and begins to question everything she's been taught.

## The Truth

The Cataclysm wasn't caused by irresponsible research. It was **sabotage**. Morven and his faction deliberately destabilized the dimensional portal to seize control of the research team and the academy's power. The result was far worse than intended, but Morven used the catastrophe to build the Order's authority.

Archmage Solara's research was actually close to breakthroughs that could have revolutionized magic in safe ways. Her death, and the destruction of her work, set magical understanding back by decades.

The three void compasses created by Solara's team still exist. One (the Thornwell Compass) has found Elara. The others are hidden or lost. Together, they may hold the key to either repeating the Cataclysm—or preventing the next one.

## Role in Story

The Cataclysm's true history is the central secret of the story. As Elara learns what really happened, she must choose between the Order (her home and family) and the truth (which means the Order is built on lies). Her choice will determine whether the kingdom moves toward healing or continues down a path of repression and resistance.
