## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, scholarly sanctuary for novelists and story architects, fusing the tactile gravitas of physical bookmaking with the surgical speed and structural discipline of elite modern software (Linear, Notion, Ulysses).

The visual aesthetic moves decisively away from sterile, bleached enterprise tools and raw, rudimentary HTML forms. Instead, it embodies **Literary Modernism**: warm ivory parchment and velvet ink surfaces, architectural hairline rules, deliberate baseline rhythm, and tactile book-object metaphors.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Authors & Worldbuilders:** Evokes deep focus, intellectual calm, reverence for the written word, and relief from visual noise.
- **Tone:** Contemplative, authoritative, warm, and surgically precise.
- **Design Philosophy:** Chrome retreats into the background to elevate the manuscript; color is semantic and directional, never decorative noise.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural book pigments: bleached linen, warm parchment, sunlit library oak, dried sepia ink, and obsidian midnight bindings.

### Surface Architecture
- **Light Mode:** Canvas begins on warm parchment (`#F6F3ED`), quiet chrome panels sit on `#FDFCF9`, and active working surfaces elevate to crisp linen card `#FFFFFF`.
- **Dark Mode ("Midnight Ink"):** Root ground rests on `#16140F`, chrome panels on warm charcoal `#1C1914`, and elevated interactive cards on `#211F1A`.
- **Borders & Rules:** Hairline 1px borders (`#E0DEDA` / `#35332D`) establish crisp structural containment without visual weight. Internal item separators use the softer `#EEEBE5` / `#302D28`.

### Functional Hues
- **Primary:** Deep Terracotta Sepia (`#743B1F` in light, luminous Amber `#D39562` in dark). Dedicated strictly to the focal CTA per view.
- **Area Navigation Tokens:** Area-specific hues (`area-writing`, `area-knowledge`, `area-chat`, `area-planning`) belong exclusively to left-rail icons and section badges to establish instant cognitive wayfinding. They never bleed into full button fills or body copy.
- **States:** Diagnostic state colors (`success`, `warning`, `danger`) are strictly reserved for consistency checks, diff comparisons, and validation alerts.

## Typography

The typography operates on a deliberate dual-engine architecture:

1. **The Prose Engine (`EB Garamond`):** Handcrafted for long-form immersion. Body manuscript lines run at 18px with a 1.75 relative line height (31.5px baseline rhythm) bounded by a strict reading measure of 34rem (544px, yielding 65–75 characters per line). Headings use authentic static 600 weight cuts. Blockquotes inherit a 2px left sepia border with warm italic phrasing.
2. **The Shell & Chrome Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** High-precision, modern humanist sans delivering razor-sharp clarity across dense data cards, chapter trees, docks, and command interfaces.

### Hierarchy Rules
- Every typographic step binds its leading directly to eliminate unwanted vertical shifts.
- Micro-labels, book spine genres, and panel section indicators use `eyebrow-tracked` with forced uppercase styling and expanded tracking.
- Numerals in metadata tables, token counts, and chapter words must utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter across editing sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances high-density operational sidebars with generous, distraction-free creative sanctuaries.

### Three-Column Workbench Layout
- **Left Icon Rail (`rail-collapsed: 48px`):** Anchors navigation with vertical tool icons. On pointer hover or focus-within, it glides outward to `rail-expanded: 168px` as an elevated overlay without pushing the central document column.
- **Chapter / Explorer Tree (240px–280px):** Sub-level hierarchy showing scenes, word-count progress, and drag-and-drop structural controls.
- **Center Canvas (Fluid with 34rem floor):** Centered manuscript canvas guaranteeing optimal reading comfort.
- **Right Inspector Dock (`dock-width: 336px`):** Houses the Consistency Inspector, AI dialogue, Lore entity graph, and scene timeline.

### Viewport Adaptation & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Full three-column view visible simultaneously.
- **Laptop (1024px – 1279px):** Central editor retains primary measure; chapter tree and inspector collapse into quick-toggle panels.
- **Tablet & Mobile (< 1024px):** Docks and navigation transform into sliding sheet drawers. The editor column maintains a 320px minimum width floor with padding scaling down from `space-xl` to `space-md`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through subtle tonal transitions, refined borders, and warm ambient shadows rather than harsh drop-shadows.

### Layer Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Root Page Ground):** Deep parchment (`#F6F3ED` / `#16140F`).
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Chrome Rails):** Flat `#FDFCF9` / `#1C1914` bound by hairline 1px `border-light` or `border-dark`. No shadow.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Cards & Editor Sheet):** `#FFFFFF` / `#211F1A` surface with 1px border and warm ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.03)` (Light Mode) or
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)` (Dark Mode).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Menus & Command Palette):** High-level overlay surfaces with 40% ink scrim backdrop blur (8px), bound by a 1px border and a deep, soft elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(36, 33, 28, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(36, 33, 28, 0.08)`.

### Border Discipline
Decorative box-shadows are strictly forbidden on standard list items and form elements. Structural definition is always asserted via hairline 1px borders and selective background washes (`bg-soft`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, slightly rounded shape geometry (Level 1: Soft) that preserves the crisp, rectangular feel of bound paper, printed journals, and architectural drafts.

- **Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** 4px (`0.25rem`) roundedness. Delivers clean, purposeful corners that harmonize with dense UI layouts.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for book cards, dialog frames, and dock panels.
- **Chips, Badges & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to create clear differentiation between interactive containers and contextual metadata pills.
- **Book Cover Geometry:** Hard-edged physical proportion adhering to an authentic `3:4` vertical aspect ratio with an embossed 5px left spine treatment and 1px inset margin.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta fill (`#743B1F` / `#D39562`), text `#FAF8F5` / `#1D140D`, 28px height, 4px radius. Focus visible: 2px ring in amber (`#8F4622` / `#F2AD73`) with 1px offset.
- **Secondary:** Surface fill `#FFFFFF` / `#211F1A`, 1px border `#E0DEDA` / `#35332D`, text `#24211C` / `#DFDEDA`. Hover: background shifts to `bg-soft`.
- **Ghost:** Borderless, text muted, hover: `bg-soft` fill and text switches to foreground.
- **Destructive:** Bordered in `#AC3031`, subtle tint on hover, clear warning feedback.

### Form Inputs & Select Controls
- Native unstyled HTML widgets are strictly banned.
- Custom input shells: fixed 28px height, 1px border `#E0DEDA` / `#35332D`, background `#FFFFFF` / `#211F1A`, horizontal padding 8px.
- Focus: replaces default browser ring with `outline: none`, border color `#8F4622` and a warm 2px amber glow ring.
- Select elements: custom chevron SVG aligned right, uniform 28px height, structured option menus matching palette surfaces.

### Book Covers & Shelf Cards
- Procedural styling based on tone:
  - **Sepia:** Deep terracotta ground, 1px gold inset frame at 5px offset, 5px left embossed spine strip, bottom-aligned title in EB Garamond.
  - **Pale:** Stone linen ground, top-left uppercase tracked eyebrow, bottom-aligned title.
  - **Ink:** Midnight charcoal ground, subtle 60% opacity metadata, centered title.
- Card Footer: Sub-metadata row displaying chapter count, word count in tabular figures, and status pill.

### AI Consistency Inspector & Finding Cards
- Structured diagnostic card with a 1px border, top highlight rule indicating severity (`warning` amber or `danger` rust).
- Displays clear conflict title (e.g. *"Forschungsstation: 7 gegen 9"*), side-by-side quote excerpts from earlier chapters, and an action bar with two explicit choices: **Übernehmen** (Primary Terracotta) and **Verwerfen** (Secondary Ghost).

### Ghost Text Streaming (Inline AI Assistant)
- In-flight AI completions stream directly into the prose editor styled with 50% opacity and a warm sepia tint.
- Trailing keystroke badge: `[Tab]` to accept, `[Esc]` to dismiss.

### Command Palette (⌘K)
- Centered modal sheet with top offset (10vh), width 576px (max-w-xl).
- Zero-border search field with live focus, categorized search sections (*Kapitel*, *Wissens-Graph*, *Szenen*, *Befehle*), and keyboard navigation hints embedded in physical keycap badges.
