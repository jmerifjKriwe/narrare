## UUID strategy decision

**`gen_random_uuid()`** — verified available on the project's PostgreSQL:
`SELECT version()` → `PostgreSQL 17.10`; `SELECT gen_random_uuid()` returns a UUID.
It is a PG 13+ builtin (no pgcrypto needed) and is already relied upon by
migrations 0013/0014/0022/0028/0031 and ORM `server_default`s (evidence/prompts
models). The plan's `uuid4()` fallback was therefore not needed. Documented in the
migration docstring.
