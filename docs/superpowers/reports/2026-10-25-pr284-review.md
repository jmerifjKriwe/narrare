## Findings

### Critical

**C1 — a provider death is indistinguishable from a completed answer, on both SSE surfaces; this
PR certifies the unreachable branch with a fabricated fixture. [inherited seam, new test]**
`app/capabilities/writing/router.py:54` and `app/capabilities/chat/router.py:64` emit
`event: error\ndata: {}\n\n`. `web/lib/stream.ts:30-33` yields only lines beginning with
`data: ` and drops the SSE `event:` field, so that frame parses to `{}` — and so does the
`done` frame. The `if (frame.error)` branches at `ChapterEditor.tsx:149` and `ChatPanel.tsx:61`
cannot fire: a truncated continuation stays on screen as a finished ghost and can be accepted
with Tab into the manuscript, and a half answer is presented as complete. Neither the alert nor
the `editor.ghostFailed` announcement the cleanup strand added can ever speak of it.
The seam itself is main's (`web/routes/chat.tsx:44`, `web/components/ChapterEditor.tsx:50`, same
routers); what this branch adds is the announcement paths that depend on it and, worse, the test
that appears to cover it: `ChapterEditor.test.tsx:34` fabricates `data: {"error": ...}` frames,
a shape no endpoint emits.

*Executed* (`/tmp/opencode/pr284-probe`, pristine `d309302e`): feeding the routers' exact bytes
to `readFrames` yields `[{"delta":"Es war"},{}]`; asserting that some frame carries `error`
fails, and error and done frames compare equal. Patching the reader to honour `event:` turns
both green (applied, then reverted).
*Fix (5 lines):* teach `readFrames` the `event:` field (or move the failure into the payload on
the server); then feed the client test the server's real bytes. This closes the inherited bug and
makes the new test honest in one change.

### Important — inherited from main (the branch's claims rest on them)

- **I1 — `app/capabilities/chat/web/ChatPanel.tsx:66-71`:** a mid-answer network failure is

## Verdict

**Mergeable after two small fixes; nothing the branch introduces is a regression of `main`.**

Before merge:

1. **C1 — fix the seam, not the test.** `web/lib/stream.ts` learns the `event:` field (5 lines),
   and `ChapterEditor.test.tsx:34` feeds the server's real bytes. Merging a green test that
   asserts a branch no server response can reach is the one thing worse than the inherited bug:
   it certifies the gap. This also closes I1's user-visible outcome on the chat surface.
2. **Two PR-body sentences** — 142, and the "no new rule or registry" wording (state the
   extension: generated views and dock-convention guards in `scripts/generate_capabilities.py`,
   no new recipe, hook or CI job).
3. **I5, or a written acceptance** — the Apache group ships Adobe's copyright for every
   package. Fixing it is a per-package copyright line; if it is accepted as-is, say so in the
   PR body rather than leaving the artifact to imply otherwise.

Carried with named owners: I4 (jobs lane — stranded `running` row), I1–I3 and the two autosave
defects (shell, inherited), and the minors above. No new gate, hook or recipe is proposed by
this review.

**For scale:** 142 backend, 32 unit and 45 E2E tests pass, and the failure this review found is
one no fake can produce — a provider dying mid-stream, which the E2E fake never does. The green
gates were accurate about everything they measured; they simply did not measure the seam that
matters here.
