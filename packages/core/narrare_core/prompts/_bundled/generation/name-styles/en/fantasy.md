### Fantasy Naming Conventions

Fantasy names draw from mythological, archaic, and invented linguistic roots.

**Phonetic Patterns:**
- Melodic consonants: l, r, m, n, s, th
- Flowing vowels: ae, ia, ie, ei, ou
- Common suffixes: -iel, -wen, -dor, -mir, -ath, -eon

**Character Names:**
- Elves: ethereal, melodic (Aerithiel, Lómion, Cerindra)
- Dwarves: guttural, compound (Thordak, Grimjaw, Boulderclef)
- Humans: varied by region (Aldric, Seraphina, Kael Stormborn)
- Orcs/Goblins: harsh consonants (Gruznak, Skraal, Throm)

**Place Names:**
- Grand, evocative compounds (Silverfall, Thornkeep, Misthollow)
- Ancient-sounding (Eldoria, Kalanthor, Vespera)

**Avoid:**
- Modern real-world names
- Excessive apostrophes (limit to 1 per name)
- Unpronounceable consonant clusters
