### Modern Naming Conventions

Modern names follow real-world cultural patterns from diverse backgrounds.

**General Guidelines:**
- Use authentic naming patterns from the specified cultural origin
- Reflect contemporary naming trends (surnames as first names, diverse origins)
- Consider generational naming patterns

**Character Names:**
- Western European: traditional and modern variants (James, Sophie, Liam, Clara)
- Eastern European: patronymic influences, distinctive consonants (Viktor, Natalia, Andrei)
- East Asian: family-name-first convention, meaningful characters (Wei, Yuki, Min-jun)
- Middle Eastern: rich historical roots, meaningful names (Amir, Fatima, Tariq)
- African: diverse regional patterns, meaningful names (Kwame, Amara, Chidi)
- Latin American: Catholic influence, compound names (Carlos, María José, Diego)

**Place Names:**
- Reflect local geography and history
- Mix of descriptive and historical names
- Consider naming conventions by region

**Guidelines:**
- Avoid cultural stereotypes or caricatures
- Research authentic naming patterns for the specified culture
- Consider the time period and social context
- Use gender-appropriate naming conventions for the specified culture
