### Science Fiction Naming Conventions

Sci-fi names blend technological, alien, and futuristic naming patterns.

**General Guidelines:**
- Names may incorporate alphanumeric elements, but remain pronounceable
- Corporate and institutional naming conventions (designations, serial codes)
- Alien names use non-standard phonetic patterns

**Character Names:**
- Colonists: compound or adapted names (Vance-Meridian, Oakley Rex)
- Corporate: designation-style names (Agent-7, Operative Kira, Dr. Chen-Sato)
- Alien: unusual phonetic combinations (Xyrath, Velnori, Threx)
- AI/Synthetic: clinical or serial designations (ARIA-7, Unit 342, NEXUS)
- Military: rank + surname or call signs (Commander Hale, "Cipher" Okafor)

**Place Names:**
- Space stations: functional + code (Station Prometheus, Outpost Delta-9)
- Planets: evocative or catalog designations (Kepler-442b, New Meridian, Ashfall)
- Ships: registry + name (ISS Vanguard, TCS Nightingale, CSV Harvest)

**Avoid:**
- Pure random letter combinations
- Names too similar to existing well-known sci-fi characters
- Excessive numbers or symbols in names
- Breaking pronounceability entirely
