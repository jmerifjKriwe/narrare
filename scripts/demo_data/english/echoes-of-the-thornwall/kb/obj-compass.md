# The Thornwell Compass

An ancient artifact that responds to void magic and stabilizes spatial distortion.

## Appearance

A handheld compass made of a dark, unknown metal that seems to absorb light. The face doesn't show directions—instead, a needle of soft blue light floats within, moving independently of physical orientation. The compass is warm to the touch and sometimes seems to pulse with an inner rhythm.

## Abilities

**Void Detection**: The compass points toward sources of void magic. The stronger the source, the more intensely the needle glows. This makes it invaluable for finding void mages, void artifacts, or areas of void resonance.

**Spatial Stabilization**: In areas of spatial distortion (like Thornwall Ruins), the compass can temporarily stabilize reality around the holder. This allows safe passage through areas that would otherwise be impassable.

**Void Communication**: The compass can be used to send simple signals to other void-resonant objects or people. The exact mechanism is unclear, but Elara learns to use it instinctively.

**Memory Echoes**: In places of strong void activity, the compass sometimes shows images of the past—fragments of what happened in that location.

## Origins

The compass was created five hundred years ago by the founders of the Temple of Silent Flames. They made three such artifacts to help mages work with void magic safely. Over centuries, two were lost or destroyed. This compass was hidden in Thornwall Academy's archives, where Elara found it during her expedition.

## Bond with Elara

The compass has formed a bond with Elara, though neither fully understands it. It responds to her touch more strongly than to anyone else's. Sometimes it moves without her consciously willing it—as if it has its own purpose or is guided by something she doesn't perceive.

## Secrets

The compass is more than a navigation tool. It's a key—literally. When combined with the other two original artifacts (if they still exist), it can unlock something significant. The Temple of Silent Flames' records hint at what, but the relevant pages were destroyed.

The compass also seems to be connected to Elara's power. When she uses void magic, the compass glows in response. When she's in danger, it activates on its own. The bond may be empowering her magic in ways she hasn't yet realized.

## Role in Story

The compass is both a tool and a mystery. It helps Elara navigate Thornwall Ruins, find other void users, and communicate with allies. But it also raises questions: What are the other two artifacts? What can they unlock together? Why has it bonded with Elara specifically?

## Symbolism

The compass represents guidance, truth-seeking, and finding one's own path. For Elara, who has always followed the Order's direction, the compass offers something new: guidance that comes from within, not from authority.
