# Cataclysm History

The magical disaster forty years ago that shaped the current world.

## Official Story

**What Happened**: Forty years ago, void magic researchers at Thornwall Academy attempted an experiment beyond their capabilities. The resulting catastrophe destroyed the academy, leveled half the capital, and killed thousands.

**Aftermath**: The Order of the Silver Flame rose from the ashes, promising to prevent such a disaster from ever happening again. They've governed magical matters ever since.

**Moral**: Uncontrolled magic is dangerous. The Order is necessary to protect society.

## Hidden Truth

**The Real Cause**: The Cataclysm wasn't an accident—it was sabotage. High Chancellor Morven and his faction deliberately destabilized the void experiment, hoping to weaponize the resulting power. When it went wrong, they blamed "reckless research" to justify their takeover.

**The Cover-up**: Evidence of sabotage was suppressed or destroyed. Survivors who knew the truth were silenced. The Order built its power on a lie.

**Motivation**: Morven discovered that dimensional travel was possible through void magic. He wanted this power for conquest and control. Archmage Solara refused. Morven killed her to get it—but didn't understand what he was tampering with.

## The Entity

**What Came Through**: When reality collapsed, something from the void slipped through. Not evil, but alien—an entity of pure curiosity that wanted to understand this reality.

**Current Status**: The entity remains trapped in the ruins of Thornwall, contained in the reality distortion. It has been speaking to Morven for forty years, promising power in exchange for release.

**Morven's Plan**: The entity needs a vessel—a void-sensitive host strong enough to contain it. Morven has been searching for forty years. He believes Elara is that vessel.

## Personal Impact

**Elara's Past**: Elara's village was destroyed in the Cataclysm. She was orphaned at age seven. The Order took her in, claiming to protect her—but they were actually looking for void-sensitive children to study and potentially use.

**The Connection**: Elara's void magic comes from the same source as the Cataclysm. She is uniquely connected to the entity in the ruins. The Thornwell Compass responds to her because it was created to work with void magic—the same magic that caused the disaster.

## The Future

**Morven's Goal**: Use Elara as a vessel for the entity, gaining its power and knowledge.

**The Resistance's Goal**: Stop Morven, expose the truth, and create a world where magic is understood rather than feared.

**Elara's Choice**: She can let Morven use her, destroy herself to prevent it, or find a third option—work with the entity willingly, on her own terms, to change everything.

The Cataclysm wasn't the end of the story. It was the beginning of Elara's.
